hi, welcome to the page of my upcoming game Rolling Mayhem X (RMX), a game that has been in development since June 2026, with its main ideas going all the way back to 2021.

This game is a fast paced platformer where you: the player, controls a rolling ball on the screen that moves automatically, and you use it to navigate levels by jumping, walljumping, dashing and more!!!

After each level you get a ranking of how well you did, ranging from F, E, D, C, B, A, S and P similar to other games probably, your goal is to get the highest ranks you possibly can, thus if you haven't gotten all P ranks yet, there's always more to practice, on top of that each P gives a special prize!!!

My goal for this game is to finish at least 8 worlds, with each world having probably 4-6 levels with the last one being a boss level

The game also has lore but i don't want to bother explaining it right now as it isn't fully thought out yet
